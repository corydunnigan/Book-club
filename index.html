About the book

Case is a former computer hacker whose damaged nervous system has left him cut off from cyberspace. Given one last chance to return to the Matrix, he is recruited for a dangerous job involving a powerful artificial intelligence. Gibson's 1984 novel is a landmark cyberpunk science-fiction story, blending high technology, crime and a dark vision of the digital future.
